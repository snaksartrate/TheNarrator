# Human rating rubric (1-5, integers)

Stories are unconditional (no prompt). Judge each on its own. 2 and 4 are intermediate.
Do not reward length alone. Rate each criterion independently. Comments optional.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar | Mostly broken words/sentences, hard to read | Understandable, frequent errors | Almost no spelling/grammar errors |
| Coherence | Sentences unrelated to each other | Mostly linked, some jumps | Each sentence follows logically |
| Creativity | Empty or purely generic filler | Ordinary but sensible idea | Interesting, surprising yet sensible idea |
| Continuity | Characters/places/events change or contradict | Minor inconsistencies | Characters and events stay consistent throughout |
| Relevance | No identifiable premise or theme | Premise present but drifts | One clear premise/theme kept to the end |
| Completeness | Cuts off, no structure | Beginning and middle, weak ending | Clear beginning, middle and ending |

Truncation at the 300-token limit counts against Completeness. Do not guess which model wrote a story.
